Root cause

The same money fact is computed in two places that drifted apart, and nothing checks that they agree. Locked tests that assert the buggy behaviour make the drift look correct.

  • Balance button vs CLI ledger walk → 135K became 7K (FP-203)
  • Capture fee vs settlement fee → off by a fil; discount credited twice on replay
  • Analytics rebuild vs reporting worker → refunds vanish
  • Webhook delivery writing payout records → duplicates, or none at all
  • Agent files ("amounts are cents", "windows are UTC") vs ADR-0001/0007
